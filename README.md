## What This Is

Bear Point Protocol is a replicable, deployable evidence vault and multi-vector pressure architecture for holding corrupt institutions accountable — school districts, government agencies, corporations, law enforcement, healthcare systems, anyone who harms people and hides behind bureaucracy.

It was built by a construction superintendent with no law degree, no funding, and a dictation mic. It works.

## How It Works

The Bear Point Protocol — formerly S.W.A.R.M. (Systemic Warfare Ancestral Reconciliation Movement) — is a methodology for applying simultaneous, compounding institutional pressure across every available vector until settlement or systemic change becomes cheaper than continued resistance.
The methodology:

1. **Evidence Architecture** — Every document hashed (SHA-256), cataloged, and cross-referenced. Chain of custody from day one. Born-digital forensics. Spoliation tracking. The institution's own records become the weapon.

2. **Compound Pressure** — File simultaneously across every available channel: federal agencies (OCR, DOJ, FPCO), state agencies (OSPI, HRC, AG), criminal referrals (local PD, prosecutor), insurance regulators, tribal sovereignty channels, professional licensing boards, legislative contacts. Each vector compounds the others. They can't consolidate, can't dismiss, can't make it go away.

3. **Communications Warfare** — Every email tracked. Every deadline monitored. Every failure to respond documented and added to the penalty clock. Delinquency becomes evidence.

4. **Pattern Recognition** — Cross-case database links institutional behavior across targets. One family's evidence strengthens another family's case. The pattern proves the practice.

5. **Settlement Engineering** — The goal is never litigation. The goal is making continued resistance more expensive than doing the right thing. Blast radius, not precision strikes.

## Deploy a New Case

```powershell
.\Deploy-Case.ps1 -CaseName "Smith_v_District" -Complainant "Jane Smith" -Victim "A.S." -Defendant "Springfield School District"
```

One command. 37 directories. Pre-populated templates for federal complaints, state agency filings, criminal referrals, PRA/FOIA requests, tort notices, and tribal outreach. Evidence registries. Actor profiles. Deadline trackers. Pressure vector dashboards. Ready to fight in under 60 seconds.

## The Methodology

Bear Point Protocol operates on a simple principle: **institutions respond to cost, not conscience.**

A single complaint gets filed in a drawer. Ten simultaneous complaints across four jurisdictions — federal, state, tribal, and criminal — each referencing the others, each compounding penalties, each creating independent discovery obligations — that gets a phone call from the general counsel.

**This is not about being right. You're already right. This is about making it impossible for them to pretend you don't exist.**
