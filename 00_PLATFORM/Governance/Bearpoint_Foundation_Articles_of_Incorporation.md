## Purpose of Corporation
To advance the civil rights of Indigenous peoples and families through research, education, community advocacy, and the coordination of legal resources; to develop and distribute replicable accountability frameworks for families facing institutional discrimination in public education and child welfare systems; and to support tribal sovereignty and cultural preservation.

---

## Other Provisions (IRS Required Language)
This corporation is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. No part of the net earnings shall inure to the benefit of any private individual. No substantial part of the activities shall consist of carrying on propaganda or attempting to influence legislation.

---

## Distribution of Assets (Dissolution Clause)
Upon dissolution, all remaining assets shall be distributed exclusively to one or more organizations that qualify as exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or to a federal, state, or local government for a public purpose. No assets shall be distributed to any director, officer, or private individual.
