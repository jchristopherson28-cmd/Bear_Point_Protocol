# BYLAWS OF BEARPOINT FOUNDATION
## A Washington Nonprofit Corporation
### EIN: 41-5180172
### Adopted: March 29, 2026

---

## ARTICLE I — NAME AND PURPOSE

### Section 1.1 — Name
The name of this corporation is **Bearpoint Foundation** (the "Foundation").

### Section 1.2 — Purpose
To advance the civil rights of Indigenous peoples and families through research, education, community advocacy, and the coordination of legal resources; to develop and distribute replicable accountability frameworks for families facing institutional discrimination in public education and child welfare systems; and to support tribal sovereignty and cultural preservation.

### Section 1.3 — Mission Lock
The purpose stated in Section 1.2 may not be amended, restated, or materially altered except by unanimous written consent of all sitting Mission Guardian Council members as defined in Article III. No action by the Board of Directors alone shall be sufficient to modify the Foundation's stated purpose.

### Section 1.4 — Nonpartisan Operation
The Foundation shall not participate in or intervene in any political campaign on behalf of or in opposition to any candidate for public office. The Foundation shall not devote a substantial part of its activities to attempting to influence legislation.

## ARTICLE II — MEMBERSHIP

### Section 2.1 — Sole Member
The Foundation shall have one (1) class of membership. Jesse M. Christopherson ("Founder") is the sole Member of the Foundation. The Member is not a director or officer by virtue of membership alone, though the Member may simultaneously hold such positions.

### Section 2.2 — Member Powers
The sole Member shall hold the following exclusive powers, which may not be delegated to the Board of Directors or any officer:

(a) Elect and remove all members of the Board of Directors, with or without cause;
(b) Elect and remove all members of the Mission Guardian Council, except as restricted by Article III;
(c) Approve or veto any amendment to these Bylaws;
(d) Approve or veto any merger, dissolution, or sale of substantially all Foundation assets;
(e) Approve or veto executive compensation exceeding the salary band established under Article IX;
(f) Approve or veto any related-party transaction as defined in Article X.

### Section 2.3 — Transfer of Membership
Membership in the Foundation is non-transferable and non-assignable. Membership may not be sold, gifted, bequeathed, or conveyed to any person or entity. Membership terminates only upon the death, permanent incapacity, or voluntary written resignation of the Member.

### Section 2.4 — Succession of Membership
Upon the death, permanent incapacity, or voluntary resignation of the Founder:
(a) All Member powers shall transfer to the Mission Guardian Council acting by unanimous vote;
(b) The Mission Guardian Council shall, within ninety (90) days, either: (i) appoint a successor Member from among persons who have demonstrated sustained commitment to the Foundation's mission and Indigenous civil rights, or (ii) convert the Foundation to a non-member nonprofit governed solely by the Mission Guardian Council;
(c) No successor Member may be appointed who has a financial interest in any entity that contracts with the Foundation;
(d) If the Mission Guardian Council fails to act within ninety (90) days, the Foundation shall be governed by the Board of Directors subject to the mission lock provisions of Section 1.3, and the Mission Guardian Council shall retain its veto powers under Article III.

### Section 2.5 — No Compensation for Membership
The Member shall receive no compensation for service as Member. The Member may be reimbursed for reasonable expenses incurred in the performance of Member duties upon presentation of documentation.

## ARTICLE III — MISSION GUARDIAN COUNCIL

### Section 3.1 — Establishment and Purpose
There is hereby established a Mission Guardian Council ("MGC") whose sole purpose is to protect the Foundation's mission, prevent organizational capture, and ensure that the Foundation's resources serve Indigenous peoples and families rather than private interests.

### Section 3.2 — Composition
The MGC shall consist of three (3) seats:

(a) **Founder Seat** — Held by Jesse M. Christopherson for life or until voluntary resignation. This seat may not be removed by any vote of any body. Upon vacancy, this seat converts to a Legacy Seat filled by the procedures in Section 2.4.

(b) **Tribal Seat** — Held by a representative nominated from a federally recognized tribal nation. The Tribal Seat holder must be an enrolled member of a federally recognized tribe. Initial appointment by the Founder; subsequent appointments by majority vote of the remaining MGC members. Term: four (4) years, renewable without limit. Removal only by unanimous vote of the other two MGC members plus the sole Member (if the Member is not also an MGC member).

(c) **Community Seat** — Held by a person with demonstrated commitment to Indigenous civil rights who is not a current Board member or Foundation employee. Initial appointment by the Founder; subsequent appointments by majority vote of the remaining MGC members with approval of the sole Member. Term: four (4) years, renewable without limit. Removal only by unanimous vote of the other two MGC members plus the sole Member.
### Section 3.3 — MGC Powers (The Kill Switch)
The MGC shall hold exclusive veto authority over the following actions. No action listed below shall be valid without affirmative vote of at least two (2) of three (3) MGC members, one of whom must be the Founder Seat holder while that seat is occupied:

(a) Any amendment to the Foundation's Articles of Incorporation or purpose statement;
(b) Any amendment to these Bylaws;
(c) Any merger, consolidation, or dissolution of the Foundation;
(d) Any sale, lease, or transfer of substantially all Foundation assets;
(e) The establishment or modification of executive compensation bands under Article IX;
(f) Any single expenditure or contract exceeding twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000);
(g) The creation of any new Board seats beyond those authorized in Article IV;
(h) Any related-party transaction as defined in Article X;
(i) Any change to the Foundation's tax-exempt status or filing of amended IRS applications;
(j) The hiring or termination of the Executive Director.

### Section 3.4 — Founder Veto
While the Founder Seat is occupied, no action under Section 3.3 shall be valid without the affirmative vote of the Founder Seat holder. This provision may not be amended during the Founder's lifetime without the Founder's written consent.

### Section 3.5 — Anti-Capture Provision
No person who holds a compensated position with the Foundation, or who has held such a position within the preceding twenty-four (24) months, may serve on the MGC. No person with a financial interest in any entity that contracts with or receives grants from the Foundation may serve on the MGC. If an MGC member develops such a conflict during their term, they shall resign the MGC seat within thirty (30) days or be deemed to have vacated the seat automatically.

### Section 3.6 — MGC Compensation
MGC members shall receive no compensation for service on the Council. Reasonable travel expenses for MGC meetings may be reimbursed upon documentation.

### Section 3.7 — MGC Meetings
The MGC shall meet at least once annually. Special meetings may be called by any MGC member or by the sole Member upon seven (7) days written notice. Meetings may be held in person or by electronic means. A quorum shall consist of all three (3) seated members. If a seat is vacant, the remaining two (2) members constitute a quorum but may only act on matters necessary to fill the vacancy and conduct routine business.

## ARTICLE IV — BOARD OF DIRECTORS

### Section 4.1 — Authority
The Board of Directors ("Board") shall manage the day-to-day affairs of the Foundation within the policies, budgets, and mission parameters established by the MGC and the sole Member.

### Section 4.2 — Composition
The Board shall consist of no fewer than three (3) and no more than five (5) directors. The creation of additional Board seats beyond five (5) requires MGC approval under Section 3.3(g). The Founder may serve simultaneously as a director.

### Section 4.3 — Election and Removal
Directors are elected by the sole Member. Directors may be removed by the sole Member with or without cause at any time. Directors serve terms of three (3) years, staggered so that no more than two (2) terms expire in the same year. Directors may be reappointed without limit.

### Section 4.4 — Anti-Packing Provision
No single vote or action shall increase the size of the Board by more than one (1) seat. No Board expansion shall take effect until sixty (60) days after MGC approval, during which the sole Member may exercise veto.

### Section 4.5 — Board Meetings
The Board shall meet at least quarterly. Special meetings may be called by the Chair, the sole Member, or any two (2) directors. A majority of seated directors constitutes a quorum. Actions require a majority vote of those present at a quorum.

### Section 4.6 — Board Compensation
Directors shall receive no compensation for service as directors. Directors who also serve as employees shall be compensated only in their employee capacity, subject to the salary band provisions of Article IX.

### Section 5.2 — Appointment and Removal
Officers are appointed by the Board with the approval of the sole Member. Officers may be removed by the Board or by the sole Member at any time with or without cause. The Executive Director (if any) may be removed only with MGC approval per Section 3.3(j).

## ARTICLE VI — FISCAL MANAGEMENT

### Section 6.1 — Fiscal Year
The fiscal year shall be the calendar year (January 1 through December 31).

### Section 6.2 — Program Spending Floor
No less than eighty percent (80%) of the Foundation's annual gross revenue shall be directed to mission-related program activities. Administrative and overhead costs shall not exceed twenty percent (20%) of annual gross revenue. This ratio shall be calculated annually and reported to the MGC.
### Section 6.3 — Expenditure Approval
(a) Expenditures under five thousand dollars ($5,000): Approved by any officer;
(b) Expenditures between $5,000 and $25,000: Approved by Board majority vote;
(c) Expenditures exceeding $25,000: Approved by Board majority vote AND MGC approval under Section 3.3(f).

### Section 6.4 — Financial Transparency
(a) The Treasurer shall prepare quarterly financial reports and present them to the Board;
(b) An annual financial report shall be prepared and provided to the MGC, all Board members, and the sole Member within ninety (90) days of fiscal year end;
(c) The annual report shall include: total revenue by source, total expenditures by category, program spending ratio, all compensation paid to officers and employees, all related-party transactions, and all expenditures exceeding $5,000;
(d) The annual financial report shall be made available for public inspection at the Foundation's principal office;
(e) Upon reaching $250,000 in annual revenue, the Foundation shall engage an independent auditor to conduct an annual audit.

### Section 6.5 — No Private Inurement
No part of the net earnings of the Foundation shall inure to the benefit of, or be distributable to, any director, officer, member, or private person, except that the Foundation shall be authorized to pay reasonable compensation for services rendered and to make payments and distributions in furtherance of its exempt purposes.

### Section 6.6 — No Loans to Directors or Officers
The Foundation shall not make any loan to any director, officer, or MGC member.

### Section 7.2 — Foundation Trademarks

The Foundation's methodology, data, case files, and research may not be sold, licensed for profit, or used to generate revenue for any private entity. Educational and training materials may be provided for fees that cover costs of delivery, but not for profit extraction.

## ARTICLE VIII — DUAL-ARM STRUCTURE

### Section 8.1 — Research and Analytics Arm
The Foundation shall maintain a research and analytics function responsible for: vault template development, pattern-and-practice database maintenance, forensic pipeline tooling, and training materials for Indigenous advocates.

### Section 8.2 — Legal and Litigation Support Arm
The Foundation shall maintain a legal support function responsible for: demand letter architecture, agency complaint coordination, tribal attorney interface and referral network, and insurance chain targeting protocols. The Foundation shall not itself practice law. Legal representation shall be provided by retained outside counsel.

## ARTICLE IX — COMPENSATION

### Section 9.1 — Salary Band
All Foundation employees shall be compensated within a defined salary band. No position shall be compensated at more than two (2) times the lowest full-time salary. The MGC shall establish the salary band annually.

### Section 9.2 — No Bonuses
No officer, director, or employee shall receive bonuses, profit-sharing distributions, or incentive compensation of any kind. Compensation shall be limited to salary and standard benefits (health insurance, retirement contributions, paid leave).
### Section 9.3 — Compensation Transparency
All salaries and benefits shall be disclosed to the full Board and MGC annually. No salary may be set or increased without Board approval. Salaries exceeding the established band require MGC approval under Section 3.3(e).

### Section 9.4 — Qualifications-Based Pay
Within the salary band, individual compensation shall be determined by qualifications, experience, and scope of responsibility. Equal work shall receive equal pay regardless of title, seniority, or personal relationship to any director, officer, or member.

### Section 9.5 — Founder Compensation
The Founder, if serving as an employee, shall be subject to the same salary band and compensation transparency requirements as all other employees. The Founder shall receive no additional compensation for service as Member or MGC member.

## ARTICLE X — CONFLICT OF INTEREST AND RELATED-PARTY TRANSACTIONS

### Section 10.1 — Definition
A "related-party transaction" is any transaction between the Foundation and: (a) any director, officer, MGC member, or employee; (b) any family member of such person; (c) any entity in which such person holds a financial interest exceeding five percent (5%); or (d) any entity that employs such person.

### Section 10.2 — Prohibition
No related-party transaction shall be entered into unless:
(a) The transaction is disclosed in full to the Board and MGC before execution;
(b) The interested party recuses from all deliberation and voting on the transaction;
(c) The Board determines by majority vote of disinterested directors that the transaction is fair, reasonable, and in the Foundation's best interest;
(d) The MGC approves the transaction under Section 3.3(h);
(e) The transaction is documented in Board minutes, including the basis for the determination of fairness.
### Section 10.3 — No Self-Dealing
No director, officer, MGC member, or employee shall use Foundation funds, property, staff, or resources for personal benefit. No such person shall direct Foundation contracts, grants, or business to entities they own or control.

### Section 10.4 — Annual Disclosure
Each director, officer, and MGC member shall submit an annual written disclosure of all financial interests, employment relationships, and family relationships that could create a conflict of interest. Failure to disclose constitutes grounds for removal.

## ARTICLE XI — DISSOLUTION

### Section 11.1 — Dissolution Procedure
The Foundation may be dissolved only by:
(a) Unanimous vote of the MGC; AND
(b) Approval of the sole Member (if membership has not been terminated under Section 2.4).

### Section 11.2 — Distribution of Assets
Upon dissolution, after payment of all liabilities and obligations, all remaining assets shall be distributed exclusively to one or more organizations exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code whose primary mission is addressing Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and People (MMIWP) or advancing the civil rights of Indigenous peoples. No assets shall be distributed to any director, officer, member, or private individual.

### Section 11.3 — Prohibition on Conversion
The Foundation may not be converted to a for-profit entity, a private foundation, or any entity that permits private inurement. Any attempted conversion is void.
---

## ARTICLE XII — AMENDMENT OF BYLAWS

### Section 12.1 — Amendment Procedure
These Bylaws may be amended only by:
(a) Approval of the sole Member; AND
(b) Approval of at least two (2) of three (3) MGC members, one of whom must be the Founder Seat holder while that seat is occupied.

### Section 12.2 — Unamendable Provisions
The following provisions may not be amended, suspended, or waived under any circumstances while the Foundation exists:
(a) Section 1.3 (Mission Lock) — except by unanimous MGC vote;
(b) Section 3.4 (Founder Veto) — may not be amended during the Founder's lifetime without Founder's written consent;
(c) Section 9.1 (Salary Band maximum ratio of 2:1);
(d) Section 10.3 (No Self-Dealing);
(e) Section 11.2 (Distribution of Assets to MMIWP/Indigenous civil rights organizations);
(f) Section 11.3 (Prohibition on Conversion);
(g) This Section 12.2.

---

## ARTICLE XIII — INDEMNIFICATION

### Section 13.1 — Indemnification of Directors and Officers
The Foundation shall indemnify any director, officer, or MGC member who was or is a party to any proceeding by reason of the fact that such person is or was a director, officer, or MGC member to the fullest extent permitted by RCW 24.03A.

### Section 13.2 — Insurance
The Foundation may purchase and maintain insurance on behalf of any person who is or was a director, officer, MGC member, or employee against any liability asserted against such person in any such capacity.

## ARTICLE XIV — ANTI-HIJACKING SUMMARY

This Article is a plain-language summary for future Board members, staff, and stakeholders. It does not create rights or obligations beyond those stated in the Articles above.

**Bearpoint Foundation was built to serve Indigenous peoples and families. These bylaws are designed to prevent the following:**

1. **Mission drift** — The purpose cannot be changed without the Founder's consent (while living) and unanimous MGC vote thereafter. (Sections 1.3, 3.3(a), 3.4)
2. **Board capture** — The sole Member elects all directors and can remove them at will. The Board cannot expand itself without MGC approval. (Sections 2.2, 4.2, 4.4)
3. **Executive enrichment** — No salary exceeds 2x the lowest full-time salary. No bonuses. No self-dealing. Full transparency. (Articles IX, X)
4. **Asset extraction** — No loans to insiders. No related-party transactions without independent review and MGC approval. 80% program spending floor. (Sections 6.2, 6.6, 10.2)
5. **Hostile dissolution** — Dissolution requires unanimous MGC vote. Assets go to MMIWP or Indigenous civil rights organizations, never to individuals. (Article XI)
6. **Conversion to for-profit** — Prohibited absolutely. (Section 11.3)
7. **Bylaws gutting** — Seven provisions are unamendable. All other amendments require both the Member and MGC. (Article XII)

**This Foundation cannot be bought, captured, converted, or gutted. It serves the people or it ceases to exist.**
