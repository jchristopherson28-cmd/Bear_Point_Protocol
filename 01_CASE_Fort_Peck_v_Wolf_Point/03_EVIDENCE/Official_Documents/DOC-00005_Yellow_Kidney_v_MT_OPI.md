# DOC-00005 — Yellow Kidney v. Montana OPI Complaint (Jul 2021)

## Overview
Class-action lawsuit filed by ACLU, ACLU of Montana, and Native American Rights Fund (NARF) challenging Montana's failure to implement Indian Education for All (IEFA) constitutional mandate.

## Parties
**Plaintiffs:** Assiniboine & Sioux Tribes of Fort Peck, Fort Belknap Indian Community, Northern Cheyenne Tribe, Little Shell Tribe, CSKT of Flathead Reservation, Crow Tribe + 18 individual student/parent/guardian plaintiffs

**Defendants:** Montana Office of Public Instruction (MOPI), Superintendent Elsie Arntzen, Montana Board of Public Education, Board Chair Darlene Schottle

## Legal Basis
- Montana Constitution Art. X § 1(2) (Indian Education clause)
- Indian Education for All (IEFA) statutes
- Declaratory and injunctive relief sought

## Key Allegations
- MOPI fails to require uniform IEFA reporting by school districts
- No monitoring of how schools use Indian education funding
- No verification that IEFA curriculum is actually taught
- No monitoring of tribal consultation in Indian education
