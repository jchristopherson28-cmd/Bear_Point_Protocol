# DOC-00001 — Fort Peck Title VI Complaint

## Overview
46-page complaint filed simultaneously with DOE Office for Civil Rights and DOJ Civil Rights Division on June 28, 2017. Filed by attorney Melina Healey (Equal Justice Works) on behalf of Fort Peck Tribal Executive Board (Assiniboine & Sioux Tribes). ACLU of Montana signed on in support.

First time tribes filed a Title VI complaint with both DOE and DOJ simultaneously on children's behalf based on school district policy.

## Allegations (from public reporting)
- Disparate discipline of Native students (2x+ suspension rate vs white peers)
- Denial of academic opportunities (AP enrollment disparity 14.6x)
- Discriminatory hiring practices (~18.5% Native staff serving 64% Native student body)
- Culturally hostile school environment
- Failure to provide adequate special education services
- Inadequate response to bullying of Native students
- Contribution to student self-harm and suicide
- Documented dozens of individual incidents
