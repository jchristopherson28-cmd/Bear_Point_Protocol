# DOC-00004 — ProPublica Miseducation Data (Wolf Point High School District)

## Overview
ProPublica's Miseducation project analyzed OCR Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC) for 96,000+ schools and 17,000+ districts. Wolf Point High School District data available at: https://projects.propublica.org/miseducation/district/3028620

## Key Findings

### AP Enrollment Disparity
- White students 14.6x more likely to be enrolled in at least one AP class than Native students
- Black, Hispanic, and Asian comparisons not available (insufficient enrollment)

### Discipline Disparity
- Native students 2x+ more likely to receive suspensions than white students
- [NEEDS VERIFICATION — exact suspension ratios from CRDC data]

### Other Reported Disparities (from tribal complaint / ProPublica reporting)
- Native students tracked into poorly funded remedial program ("Eagle Academy")
- Limited gifted/talented identification for Native students
- Inadequate special education services
