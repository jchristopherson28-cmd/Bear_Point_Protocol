# DOC-00003 — Jackson v. Wolf Point SD Consent Decree (2014)

## Overview
Consent decree resolving voting rights malapportionment case. Wolf Point School District conceded that voting districts violated the Equal Protection Clause (14th Amendment, one person one vote).

## Key Terms
- 5 single-member voting districts (replacing discriminatory 2-district system)
- 1 at-large member for high school board only
- Population variance no more than 1.54% between districts
- Implementation over 2 years

## Prior Disparity
| District | Population | Board Representation | Ratio |
|----------|------------|---------------------|-------|
| District 3 (majority white) | ~143/member | Over-represented | 1:143 |
| District 45 (majority Native) | ~841/member | Under-represented | 1:841 |
| **Disparity** | | | **5.9x** |

## Significance
- Remedied political representation but did not address educational discrimination
- Post-decree: 3/6 board members Native or Native descent by 2018
- Native board members reportedly still shut out of decision-making via secret meetings
- Voting reform necessary but insufficient — institutional culture unchanged
