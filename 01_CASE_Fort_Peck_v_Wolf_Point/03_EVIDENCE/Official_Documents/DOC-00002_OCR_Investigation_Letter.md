# DOC-00002 — OCR Investigation Letter (Dec 28, 2018)

## Overview
Letter from DOE Office for Civil Rights notifying Melina Healey (counsel for Fort Peck Tribal Executive Board) that OCR would investigate the June 2017 complaint. Sent hours after ProPublica/NYT investigation published.

## Investigation Scope (from reporting)
- Whether Wolf Point schools discipline Native students more harshly than white students
- Whether Native students are tracked into remedial programs without justification
- Whether Native students are denied access to advanced academic opportunities
- Whether special education services are inadequate for Native students
- Whether hiring practices are discriminatory
- Whether school environment is culturally hostile

## Significance
- Issued 18 months after complaint filed — dormancy broken by media publication
- Bucks DeVos-era DOE policy of pulling back from systemic discrimination investigations
- At time of letter, OCR already had separate open complaint (Louella Contreras re: Ruth Fourstar)
- WaPo reported three open investigations into Wolf Point plus two under evaluation
