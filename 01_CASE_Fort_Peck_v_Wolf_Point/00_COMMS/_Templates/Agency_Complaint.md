# AGENCY COMPLAINT TEMPLATE

> **Purpose:** File discrimination/civil rights complaints with federal and state agencies.

---

## FEDERAL — OCR (Office for Civil Rights)

**File at:** https://ocrcas.ed.gov/
**Statute:** Title VI (42 U.S.C. § 2000d), Section 504 (29 U.S.C. § 794), Title IX (20 U.S.C. § 1681)
**Deadline:** 180 days from last discriminatory act (extendable for continuing violations)

### Required Elements:
1. Complainant name, address, phone, email
2. Basis of discrimination (race, national origin, disability, sex)
3. Name and address of institution
4. Description of discriminatory act(s)
5. Date(s) of discriminatory act(s)
6. Whether complaint filed elsewhere
7. Signature + consent form

---

## FEDERAL — DOJ (Civil Rights Division)

**File at:** https://civilrights.justice.gov/report/
**Statute:** Title VI, Section 1983, Pattern-or-practice
**Note:** DOJ reviews independently — not bound by OCR outcomes.

---

## STATE — OSPI (RCW 28A.642 / WAC 392-190)

**File at:** equity@k12.wa.us
**Statute:** RCW 28A.642, WAC 392-190-065
**Deadline:** Per WAC 392-190 procedures
**District response:** 30 calendar days

### Required Elements:
1. Student name (or initials for privacy)
2. School and district
3. Specific policy/law violated
4. Dates and description
5. Remedy sought

---

## STATE — WSHRC (WA Human Rights Commission)

**File at:** https://www.hum.wa.gov/
**Statute:** RCW 49.60 (WLAD)
**Deadline:** 180 days from discriminatory act (6 months)
**Cross-file:** EEOC (employment), HUD (housing)

---

## STATE — WSBA (Attorney Grievance)

**File at:** https://www.wsba.org/for-the-public/file-a-grievance
**Statute:** RPC 4.1, 8.4(c), 8.4(d)
**Note:** Document specific RPC violations with exhibits

---

## STATE — OPP (Office of Professional Practices)

**File at:** Via ESD superintendent (WAC 181-86-110 routing)
**Statute:** WAC 181-86
**Note:** OPP may decline — ESD routing is independent statutory pathway
