# FOIA REQUEST TEMPLATE

> **Statute:** 5 U.S.C. § 552 (Freedom of Information Act)
> **Response deadline:** 20 business days (extendable 10 additional for "unusual circumstances")
> **Appeal:** Agency denial → administrative appeal → federal court (D.C. or requestor's district)

---

## TO:
FOIA Officer
{FEDERAL_AGENCY_NAME}
{AGENCY_FOIA_EMAIL}
{AGENCY_ADDRESS}

## SUBJECT:
FOIA Request — {DESCRIPTION}

---

## BODY:

Dear FOIA Officer:

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, I request access to and copies of the following records:

**Requested Records:**

1. {RECORD_DESCRIPTION_1}
2. {RECORD_DESCRIPTION_2}
3. {RECORD_DESCRIPTION_3}

**Date Range:** {START_DATE} through present.

**Case Reference:** {AGENCY_CASE_NUMBER} (if applicable)

**Fee Category:** I request a fee waiver under 5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii), as disclosure of the requested information is in the public interest because it is likely to contribute significantly to public understanding of the operations or activities of the government and is not primarily in my commercial interest.

If a fee waiver is denied, I am willing to pay fees up to ${FEE_LIMIT}. Please notify me before incurring charges beyond this amount.

**Format:** I request records in electronic format (PDF with text layer, .docx, .xlsx as applicable).

**Expedited Processing:** [If applicable] I request expedited processing under 5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(6)(E) because {URGENCY_BASIS}.

Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(6)(A), I expect a determination within 20 business days. If you determine that unusual circumstances apply, please notify me in writing per § 552(a)(6)(B).

If any responsive records are withheld in whole or in part, I request that you identify the specific exemption(s) claimed under 5 U.S.C. § 552(b) and provide a Vaughn index for any withheld material.

Respectfully,

{COMPLAINANT_NAME}
{ADDRESS}
{PHONE}
{EMAIL}

## PRIVACY ACT COMPANION (5 U.S.C. § 552a)

If requesting records about yourself or your child:

> I also request, pursuant to the Privacy Act of 1974, 5 U.S.C. § 552a, an accounting of all disclosures made of records pertaining to {COMPLAINANT_NAME / VICTIM_NAME} maintained in your systems of records, as required by § 552a(c)(3).

**Privacy Act elements:**
- Request records maintained about you/your child
- Request accounting of all disclosures to third parties
- Request correction of inaccurate records (§ 552a(d)(2))
- 14-day response for name corrections
