# Melina Healey

## Role
Attorney representing Fort Peck Tribal Executive Board on Title VI complaint. Equal Justice Works fellow. Described as specializing in Indian Law, Juvenile Law, Racial and Indigenous Justice.

## Key Facts (Public Record)
- Drafted and filed the 46-page Title VI complaint (Jun 28, 2017)
- Filed simultaneously with DOE OCR and DOJ — first time tribes filed with both agencies on children's behalf re: school policy
- Worked with ACLU of Montana as co-counsel
- Called for "swift and thorough investigation that leads to much-needed reforms" (Montana Public Radio, Jul 2017)
- Confirmed unaware of any changes by school system to address complaint allegations (WaPo, Jan 2019)
