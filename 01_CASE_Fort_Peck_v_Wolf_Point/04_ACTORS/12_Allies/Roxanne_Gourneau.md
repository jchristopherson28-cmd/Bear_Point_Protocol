# Roxanne Gourneau

## Role
Member, Fort Peck Tribal Executive Board. Lead advocate for Title VI complaint.

## Key Facts (Public Record)
- Son died by suicide after being disciplined at Wolf Point High School (~2010)
- Drove tribal complaint filing as Executive Board member
- Has worked to reform Wolf Point schools since son's death
- Quoted extensively in ProPublica/NYT investigation

## Key Quotes (Public Record)
- "The school district has done more to fuel Native students' trauma than to support their education." (ProPublica, Dec 2018)
- "We are at a do-or-die moment for our tribe's children. I don't want any more of our youngsters to end up in the cemetery or prison." (In These Times, Jul 2017)
- "A good many teachers and administrators who were around during the 2013 ACLU lawsuit have left. Those who replaced them, even those who came here from out of state, soon began engaging in the same bullying and excessive discipline of our Native children. It was like iron filings to a magnet. I was amazed and realized the problems are systemic." (In These Times)
- "Reservations all over the country face education-related discrimination, whether their children attend schools in white bordertowns or in white on-reservation enclaves." (In These Times)

## Assessment
Core advocate. Personal loss (son's suicide) → institutional advocacy → tribal complaint → federal investigation. Pattern: individual tragedy → systemic reform effort. Analogous to Jesse's trajectory in SVR.
