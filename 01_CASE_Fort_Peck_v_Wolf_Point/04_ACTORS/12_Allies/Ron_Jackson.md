# Ron Jackson

## Role
Former Wolf Point School Board member (15 years, 10 as chairman). Current warden, Fort Peck Indian Reservation adult correctional facility (appointed by Tribal Executive Board, 2016).

## Key Facts (Public Record)
- Assiniboine tribal member
- Pushed to curb discrimination against Native students during board tenure
- Fought for reading instruction, support for Native children, challenged expulsion decisions
- Consistently outvoted by white majority on board
- Lost election in 2012 — board went all-white
- Joined Jackson v. Wolf Point SD (2013) voting rights lawsuit as lead plaintiff
- Now encounters former students as inmates — direct witness to school-to-prison pipeline
- Shifted correctional facility focus from punishment to rehabilitation/education
- First act as warden: gathered inmates in prayer circle for redemption

## Assessment
Embodiment of the school-to-prison pipeline narrative. Fought discrimination from inside the system for 15 years, was outvoted, then encountered the consequences of that failure in the correctional facility. Powerful witness for systemic harm.
