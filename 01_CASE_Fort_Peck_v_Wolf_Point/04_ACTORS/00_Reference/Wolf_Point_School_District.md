# Wolf Point School District

## Overview
Public school district serving Wolf Point, Montana, the largest town on the Fort Peck Indian Reservation. Population ~2,600 (57% Native American).

## Structural Analysis
White minority controls: municipal government, local economy, school board (historically), school administration. Pattern: homesteaders opened reservation land early 20th century → descendants dominate institutions → Native majority marginalized in their own territory. Same dynamic as Yelm Community Schools but on reservation land with treaty implications.
