## RESPONSE REQUIREMENT REFERENCE

> Common deadlines for different types of filings. **Check your state's specific statutes.**

| Type | Statutory Basis | Mandatory Response Time | What Happens If They Don't Respond |
|------|----------------|------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Public records request | WA: RCW 42.56.520 | 5 business days to acknowledge | Penalties up to $100/day |
| Tort claim (district) | WA: RCW 4.96.020 | 60 days | Deemed denied — you can file suit |
| Tort claim (state) | WA: RCW 4.92.100 | 60 days | Deemed denied — you can file suit |
| State education complaint | WA: WAC 392-190-065 | 30 days (district response) | Noncompliance finding |
| OCR complaint | CPM § 108 | Varies | Federal enforcement |
| Bar grievance | State bar rules | Varies | Disciplinary proceedings |
| FOIA request (federal) | 5 U.S.C. § 552 | 20 business days | Constructive denial — federal court |

> **Note:** These are Washington State examples. Update the statutes for your state.
