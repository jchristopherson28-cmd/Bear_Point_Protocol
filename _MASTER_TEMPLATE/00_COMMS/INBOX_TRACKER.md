## GMAIL SEARCH TIPS

If you have Claude connected via Gmail MCP, you can ask Claude to search your email.
Some useful searches:

- **All emails from a person:** `from:person@email.com`
- **All emails about your case:** `subject:keyword` (use a short keyword like the school name)
- **Emails in a date range:** `after:2026/01/01 before:2026/02/01`
- **Unread case-related:** `is:unread keyword`
