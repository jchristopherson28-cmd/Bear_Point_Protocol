# TORT CLAIM NOTICE TEMPLATE

> **District (RCW 4.96.020):** 60-day notice to governing body before filing suit.
> **State (RCW 4.92.100):** 60-day notice to ORM + agency + AG before filing suit.
> **PRA claims exempt:** RCW 42.56.550 — no tort claim notice required.

---

## District Tort (RCW 4.96.020)

### TO:
{DISTRICT_NAME} Board of Directors
{SUPERINTENDENT_NAME}, Superintendent
{DISTRICT_ADDRESS}

### CC:
{INSURANCE_CONTACT}

### SUBJECT:
Notice of Claim — RCW 4.96.020

### SERVICE:
- [ ] Email: {DATE}
- [ ] Certified mail: {DATE} / Tracking: {TRACKING_NUMBER}

### BODY ELEMENTS:
1. Claimant identification (name, address, DOB)
2. Date and location of incident
3. Description of conduct causing injury
4. Nature of injuries
5. Names of involved public employees
6. Amount of damages claimed (or statement reserving)
7. Evidence preservation demand
