# FERPA RECORDS REQUEST
*Day-1 Protection Letter No. 3 of 4*

> **Purpose:** Triggers the federal 45-day response clock under 34 C.F.R. § 99.10(b). Gets the complete student record before it can be altered. Establishes the baseline that will be compared against any subsequent production. A district's failure to respond within 45 days is itself a FERPA violation reportable to the DOE Family Policy Compliance Office (FPCO).

> **Delivery:** Email to superintendent and building principal, with certified mail same day. The 45-day clock runs from the date of receipt, not the date of filing — certified mail creates a verifiable receipt date.

## I. RECORDS REQUESTED

I request copies of all education records as defined under FERPA, 34 C.F.R. § 99.3, including but not limited to:

**Academic Records:**
- All transcripts, report cards, grade records, and academic progress reports
- All course enrollment records and scheduling documents
- All standardized test scores and assessment records

**Attendance Records:**
- Complete attendance history from {START_DATE} to present
- All records of excused and unexcused absences, including any reclassification of absence status
- All attendance notifications, letters, or formal truancy-related communications

**Behavioral and Disciplinary Records:**
- All incident reports, behavioral referrals, and disciplinary records
- All records of suspensions, expulsions, or other disciplinary actions
- All records of investigations relating to {VICTIM_NAME}

**Special Education and 504 Records** *(if applicable)*:
- All IEP documents, evaluation reports, and meeting notes
- All 504 accommodation plans and review records
- All referral documentation

**Health and Counseling Records:**
- All health records maintained by the school
- All counseling session notes (subject to applicable privilege)

**Communications and Correspondence:**
- All written communications between district staff and this family
- All internal communications (emails, memos, notes) among district staff mentioning {VICTIM_NAME}

**System Records:**
- Complete student information system record for {VICTIM_NAME}, including all fields and all modification history
- All audit logs showing who accessed or modified {VICTIM_NAME}'s records, and when

---

## II. FORMAT OF PRODUCTION

**I request that all records be produced:**

1. **In native digital format** — in the format in which the records are ordinarily maintained (e.g., native Word or Excel files, not printed copies; native database records, not screenshots)
2. **With all metadata preserved** — including creation dates, modification dates, author information, and version history
3. **As text-searchable PDFs** — where records are maintained as PDFs, they must be produced with a text layer intact, not as image-only scans

> **Note on format:** Records produced as image-only scans when the originals are text-searchable documents constitute an impermissible degradation of the records. I am preserving the right to challenge any production that converts text-searchable records to image-only format.
