# POLICY INQUIRY LETTER
*Day-1 Protection Letter No. 4 of 4*

> **Purpose:** Forces the district to produce and commit to specific policy versions on record. Creates a baseline against which selective enforcement, retroactive policy changes, and procedural violations can be measured. A district that cannot produce its own policies on request reveals an enforcement problem. A district that produces one version today and cites a different version later has a documentation problem.

> **Delivery:** Email to superintendent and principal. Certified mail recommended. Five-business-day response window.

## II. ADDITIONAL QUESTIONS

1. Who is the district's designated civil rights compliance officer responsible for Title VI, Section 504, and Title IX compliance? Please provide their name, title, phone number, and email address.

2. Who is the district's FERPA records custodian? Please provide their name, title, phone number, and email address.

3. Has the district adopted WSSDA model policies or local equivalents? If local policies deviate from state model policies, please identify the deviations.

4. Are any of the policies listed above currently under revision? If so, which ones, and what is the anticipated effective date of the revised versions?

5. {If tribal enrollment is at issue:} Does the district have a memorandum of understanding or formal agreement with {TRIBAL_NATION} regarding educational services for enrolled tribal members? If so, please provide a copy.
