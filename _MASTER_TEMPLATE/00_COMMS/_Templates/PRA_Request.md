# PRA REQUEST TEMPLATE

> **Statute:** RCW 42.56.080 — Public Records Act
> **Response deadline:** 5 business days to acknowledge (RCW 42.56.520)
> **Penalty:** $100/day for noncompliance (Yousoufian v. Office of Ron Sims)

---

Public Records Act Request — {DESCRIPTION}

## BODY:

Dear {RECORDS_OFFICER_NAME},

Pursuant to Washington's Public Records Act, chapter 42.56 RCW, I am requesting access to and copies of the following public records:

**Requested Records:**

1. {RECORD_DESCRIPTION_1}
2. {RECORD_DESCRIPTION_2}
3. {RECORD_DESCRIPTION_3}

**Date Range:** {START_DATE} through present.

**Format:** I request records in their native digital format (e.g., .docx, .xlsx, .pdf with text layer intact). Pursuant to RCW 42.56.120, agencies must provide records in the format in which they are maintained. Print-to-scan conversion of born-digital documents does not satisfy this requirement.

**Identifiable Records:** These records are identifiable and locatable with reasonable effort by {AGENCY_NAME} staff.

Pursuant to RCW 42.56.520, I expect an initial response within five (5) business days of receipt providing a reasonable estimate for production, or access to requested records. Failure to respond constitutes a denial subject to statutory penalties under RCW 42.56.550(4).

I am willing to pay reasonable copying charges per RCW 42.56.120. Please notify me if charges exceed ${COST_THRESHOLD} before incurring costs.

Respectfully,

{COMPLAINANT_NAME}
{PHONE}
{EMAIL}
