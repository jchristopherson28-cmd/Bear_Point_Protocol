## KEY FRAMING:

### 1. Enrolled Member Status
- Name (initials in formal correspondence): {VICTIM_INITIALS}
- Enrollment #: {ENROLLMENT_NUMBER}
- Tribe: {TRIBE_NAME}
- Treaty: {TREATY_NAME} ({TREATY_YEAR})

### 2. Federal Trust Obligation
- Treaty obligations implicated when state entity harms enrolled member
- Federal trust responsibility (25 U.S.C. § 5301 et seq.)
- ICWA applicability (25 U.S.C. § 1901 et seq.) if child welfare/truancy proceedings initiated

### 3. Tribal Sovereignty Interests
- Independent enforcement authority
- Government-to-government dispute resolution (RCW 43.376)
- Treaty-based remedies
- Cultural harm / intergenerational impact

### 4. What the Tribe Can Do
- Invoke government-to-government consultation
- Demand ICWA compliance
- Intervene in state proceedings
- File amicus or party brief in federal proceedings
- Engage federal trust responsibility through BIA
- Exercise tribal court jurisdiction where applicable
